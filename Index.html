Account and Data Deletion

Official Account Deletion Information

MI Traders Business Suite respects your privacy. Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information at any time.

Important: Uninstalling the application does not delete your account or personal data. You must submit an account deletion request.

1. Delete Your Account Through the App

  1. Open MI Traders Business Suite.
  2. Log in using your registered account.
  3. Open your Profile or Account Settings.
  4. Select the Delete My Account option.
  5. Read the confirmation message and confirm deletion.

2. Request Account Deletion Through Email

If you cannot access the application, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

Send Deletion Request

Please include your registered email address or phone number and account type (Customer or Worker). We may request additional information to verify that you own the account.

3. What Data Will Be Deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete personal information associated with your account, as applicable, including:

  • Personal profile information.
  • Account identification information.
  • Uploaded personal profile images and media.
  • Other personal account information associated with your account.

4. Data Retention

Certain information may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or legitimate business obligations. Such information will be retained only for the period required by applicable laws and obligations.

5. Deletion Processing Time

We will verify and process account deletion requests within the applicable legal and Google Play policy requirements. If additional verification is necessary, we will contact you.
